Eclipse

A dark fantasy roleplay server for Hytale.

A dying world laid out in three rings around the last city. The further you go, the richer the earth and the closer death. Your standing is measured in renown, scars and alliances, not levels. Nobody survives alone.

The five pillars

Forced interdependence

No one completes a value chain alone. The miner needs the smelter, the smith needs both, and the alchemist's oil is what keeps a blade from rusting.

Roleplay as gameplay

You rise through renown and appointments granted by other characters. There is nothing to grind.

Weight of the world

Fog, darkness, a quiet HUD. Wounds linger, minds fray, and death has consequences.

Text over voice

Speech carries 3, 20 or 50 blocks and breaks up at the edge. No global chat. News travels by letter.

Narrative consequence

Death is a story moment, not a reset. Where you fall decides what you lose.

The ring world

The world is a circle around Last Hearth, the capital. Six highways leave its gates and decay as they run outward. Beyond the last ring rises a wall of basalt, and beyond that, nothing. Select a ring.

Fate: green · radius 0 to 1500

The Gilded Cage

Eternal autumn in a suffocating dusk.

Flat farmland under golden-grey fog, oaks and birches with dry leaves, small still ponds. The only safe place to farm. Its heart is Last Hearth, a capital built block by block by hand, with a mint, a post office, a notice board and plots for rent.

Food, wood, first herbs. No stone, no ore. Fall here and you wake in the infirmary.

Fate: orange · radius 1500 to 3000

The Rotting Weald

A diseased swamp where the corruption has taken root.

Mud-brown water, dead trees, rain and ground fog. Pools of murky poison slow and sicken the careless. Snakes and bandits by day; at night wolf packs, spiders and the dead.

Copper, iron, silver, swamp alchemy. Fall here and you live, but you keep a scar.

Fate: red · radius 3000 to 4500

The Cinder Scars

The source of the corruption. Dark, creepy, lonely.

Flat ash dunes under a near-black sky, glowing cracks, lone basalt claws. Every minute here fills your corruption. Below lie the Primordial Caverns, a withered forest of stone and bone.

Thorium, adamantite, mithril, void ingredients. Fall here and you are gone for good.

The edge · radius 4500 and beyond

The Border Mountains

A wall of basalt, and then the Void.

Scree foothills rise into cliffs of up to 300 blocks, ash on the ledges, ember veins in the rock. No one climbs them. No one builds there. What lies beyond is nothing at all.

The world ends here.

How the world bites

Every system below runs on the server today, at least in a first version.

ᚠ

Professions

A main trade and at most one second. You learn only as the apprentice of a master. Miner, smelter, smith. Farmer, cook. Herbalist, alchemist. Surgeon. Anyone else who breaks ore gets worthless rock.

ᛟ

Castes & renown

Branded, Peasantry, Specialists, Gentry, High Lords. Renown is granted for good roleplay, and promotion is an appointment by someone above you who stakes their own name on it.

ᚺ

Wounds

A heavy hit leaves a deep wound: bleeding, a broken leg, an internal injury. Anyone can bandage. Only a surgeon can set a leg or open you up. Wounds survive relog and respawn.

ᛞ

Fate

At zero health you collapse where you fell. In the Cage you wake in the infirmary. In the Weald you rise with a scar for life. In the Scars your name goes on the memorial wall.

ᛉ

Corruption

The ash fills you, minute by minute. Only the Purifying Brew, made from herbs of the safest ring, pushes it back. At full corruption the character dies.

ᛜ

Sanity

Darkness and death wear down the mind. Sleep, fire, good food and dark ale bring it back. Let it slip and you hear whispers in chat, in the names of the people standing next to you.

ᛗ

Coin & trade

Copper, silver and gold coins are items you can lose. Only the Gentry may strike them at the mint. Trade chests sell while you sleep; a notice board pays for goods and bounties.

ᚱ

Letters

There is no global chat. A letter is an item, sealed and carried by hand to a mailbox. Anyone may deliver it for the postage. Anyone may break the seal, and the recipient will know.

ᛃ

Land that renews

Unclaimed wilds that no one touches for a week grow back with fresh ore, trees and herbs. Drive a stake to keep a chunk. In the city, plots are rented by the week.

Where we stand

  1. The world

    Three biomes, caverns, border mountains, atmosphere and weather per ring, poison water, decaying roads, ruins per ring, alchemy herbs by tier, our own mob spawns per ring and time of day.

  2. The foundation

    Characters, proximity chat, renown and castes, claims, city plots and rent, permissions, an admin web app for the staff. Still to come: Last Hearth itself, built by hand.

  3. The struggle to survive

    Wounds, professions with our own recipe book, coins, trade chests, notice board, letters. Next: infection, the remaining trades, more threats.

  4. The horror

    Corruption, sanity and Fate in a first version. Next: the corrupted rising as enemies, supernatural foes, players seen as monsters.

Join the circle

Eclipse is a small project with a big world. We are looking for people who like the idea of a slow, harsh, story-driven server and want to shape it before it opens.

Testers

Walk the rings, die in them, break the systems. Many features need two or more players to test at all: treating wounds, apprenticeships, letters, trade.

Builders

Last Hearth is built by hand: walls and six gates, cathedral, keep, bazaar, infirmary, post office, taverns. Plus ruins and camps for the wilds.

Storytellers & game masters

Lore, factions, the Rot Cult, the first masters of each trade. People who want to run scenes and events once players arrive.

Artists & developers

Models and textures in the Hytale style, music for the rings. Java for the server plugins, Svelte and Go for the admin app.
